## `OPT-66-LSAT` evaluator

**Primary evidence:** text map, evidence ledger, reasoning map, alternative, conclusion / persuasive response.

**Dimensions:**

- task orientation and text segmentation;
- evidence traceability and context;
- reasoning relationship and warrant;
- alternative / uncertainty handling;
- conclusion and communication fit;
- original-source and rights compliance.

**Critical failures:** invented passage content, answer without evidence, protected LSAT material copied, or admission / legal-practice claim.

**Challenge set design:** original dense passage; ambiguous passage; attractive unsupported answer; conflicting passage pair; accessibility-variant passage.

**Improvement loop:** diagnose the missing evidence or warrant → revise prompt to require a locator or alternative → rerun on a new passage → compare evidence completeness and reasoning quality.
